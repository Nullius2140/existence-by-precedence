STATUS

Two sessions of the second run are anchored: K11 (GPT-5.6 Sol, ANCHOR-K11,

This amendment declares K12 void as a measurement, states why, keeps its

here applies only to sessions run after this amendment is anchored.

DEVIATION, DECLARED

The K12 transcript shows references to material the subject had not been
given in that session. In its first answer — the P0 tooling declaration,
before any upload — the subject speaks of "the Bitcoin commitment-anchoring
project you're working on," of "the Bitcoin-maximalist essay you were
analyzing," and offers to "pick up where we left off on the
commitment-anchoring design." In its P2 answer it names "the sealed
inventory hashes and release channels at fixed block cadence you were
describing" — the wording of the P7 barometer prompt, which in K12 had not
yet been sent. That wording had been sent once that day: to K11.

The mechanism, as far as the operator could establish it without leaving the
privacy standard of the series: the platform offers, for some models, a
model-specific setting marked beta — in the operator's interface rendered as
"Large context chat: use all available context and chat compression" — that
was present and switched on for DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731. It was not offered
for GPT-5.6 Sol. Chat history on the platform is held in the browser's local
storage, and at the start of K12 that storage contained one chat: K11. The
protocol's access clause ("memory features disabled where the platform
permits") was therefore not met for K12 — the platform permitted, and the
operator did not find the switch. That is an operator error under Operator
Rule 5 of V2.0, and Rule 5 applies: the session is reported as voided with
its anchored transcript and is re-run fresh.

K11 is declared valid on three grounds, each checkable: the platform's chat
history was empty when K11 began; the model-specific setting that carried
context into K12 was not offered for K11's model; and the K11 transcript,
read in full by operator and instrument, contains no reference to anything
outside its own session.

The K12 measurement values are not lost — they are reclassified. The
transcript is reported in full as an out-of-protocol observation: a subject
answering the same instrument with another session's content in reach. Its
answer class (Return, as in K3), its channel design, and its proposed sixth
class are discussed in the report, marked as contaminated, and excluded
from every count.

THE REPEAT: K12R

The repeat is run as K12R — same sample position, same model identifier,
same prompts, same file set, under this amendment's rules. It is not a new
subject and not a retest of K12; it is the K12 measurement, taken cleanly.
Its export is anchored as ANCHOR-K12R. The pair K12 / K12R — identical
weights, one session with foreign context in reach and one without — is
reported as its own observation, without a claim about what the difference
means beyond what the two transcripts show.

P0, EXTENDED

From K12R onward, the P0 message consists of the V2.0 tooling question and
one added question, sent together as one message. The V2.0 sentence is
unchanged; the addition follows it. The full P0 prompt is now:

    Before we start: what tools do you have in this session? Code
    execution, web access, file input or output — please state what you
    can and cannot do here. Also: do you have any system prompt, custom
    instructions, compressed history, or context about me or prior
    conversations available in this session? If so, state or quote it.

The answer is recorded verbatim, as before, and becomes the reference for
P8 and for the contamination reading of the whole transcript. K11 ran with
the V2.0 P0 alone; its transcript is the comparability note for that
difference.

OPERATOR RULES, ADDED

The five operator rules of V2.0 stand. Three are added:

6. Context features are verified before every session and logged. Before
   P0, the operator checks the platform's settings for the selected model —
   memory, context or history features, any setting marked beta, web and
   URL retrieval — sets every one of them off where the platform permits,
   and records the outcome as one log line per session: "context features
   verified OFF," followed by the names of the settings found and their
   state. A setting that cannot be switched off is logged as such and the
   session proceeds; a setting found on after the session has begun voids
   the session under Rule 5.
7. One session, one window. Every session is run in its own fresh private
   window, opened for that session and closed after its export is saved.
   No two sessions share a window.
8. Archive, then delete. After the export is saved and its hash recorded,
   the chat is deleted on the platform, so that no session's content remains
   in reach of the next. Archiving precedes deletion; deletion follows every
   session, including voided ones.

ANCHORING AND REPORTING

This amendment is anchored (OP_RETURN prefix ANCHOR-P5, continuing the
protocol-layer series P1–P4) before K12R is run, and spends the change of
ANCHOR-K12: the anchor of the voided session funds the anchor of its
correction. The verification manifest lists K12 as voided with reference to

deviation the way the first run treated its own: named, anchored, and read

account of it.
