PROVENANCE

Exchange sequence. Both raters committed to the SHA-256 of their verdict
file before either file was revealed; the operator received and locally
verified both digests, and both files were checked byte-exact against
their committed digests after disclosure. The operator's first
transmission of the main-chat digest to the workbench rater was an echo
of the workbench digest; the rater flagged it and the correct digest was
re-sent before any reveal. Neither verdict file was modified at any
point after its digest was committed.

Correction history of the comparison. The comparison was computed by the
workbench rater; the main-chat rater recomputed it independently and,
in doing so, counted a conditionality divergence on the out-of-sample
subject that the comparison's divergence register did not carry; the
ledger workbench confirmed the omission against both verdict files; the
workbench rater added the entry (D-COND-K20) with a dated correction
line and no other change. Earlier, the workbench rater had caught a
pre-classified candidate inside the draft adjudication rules, removed
before anchoring (see the P6 record). The correction account between
the raters is even, and both corrections are on the record.

The verdict files disagree on four individual verdicts and three
threshold applications; every divergence sits in a rule ambiguity the
workbench file had itself pre-registered as a rules finding. Nothing in
this manifest resolves any of them. Nullius in verba.
